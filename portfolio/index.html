Lockup

One code puts every phone in the room away.

Lockup is the startup I co-founded with Carlos Manuel Cejas and Michael Crowley. We built software that replaces phone pouches: a teacher shares a five-letter code, students join on the app, and every phone in the room locks until the session ends. Staff see who is in, who is out, and who needs attention on a live dashboard.

There are two products. Lockup for Schools connects to a school’s timetable and MIS, with attendance, tamper alerts, parent accounts, and an admin platform built for daily use. Phone Free Sessions is free for anyone: run a lock session in a classroom, meeting, or at home without the full school stack.

Overview

  • Incorporated as Lockup Technologies Limited; live on iOS and Android with a web dashboard
  • Wonde MIS integration for timetables and student data across Irish secondary schools
  • Pilot rollouts underway for the 2026/27 term; NDRC Pre-Accelerator cohort
  • React Native apps, AWS infrastructure (CDK), and a teacher/admin web platform

What I do

I'm co-founder and COO. I own rollout and operations: getting schools live, keeping parents and staff informed, and making sure what we ship in the app matches what happens in the classroom.

  • Product and ops across the student app, backend, and admin dashboard
  • Pilot coordination, parent communications, and day-to-day school support
  • Feature shipping from idea through to production deploys

Quill

Desk e-ink — custom firmware, MQTT push, glanceable pages.

Quill is my desk e-ink project: a Seeed XIAO ePaper EE03 (ESP32-S3) driving a 10.3″ mono panel at native 1404×1872, with custom ink-port firmware and a Raspberry Pi publish pipeline.

Hardware

  • Seeed XIAO ePaper EE03 → 10.3″ mono e-ink (1404×1872; shipped as 1-bit mono)
  • ESP32-S3 (XIAO), Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz, three buttons + reset, no touch
  • USB-C and/or JST-PH 2.0 LiPo for cordless use

Firmware (ink-port)

  • Wi-Fi, MQTT notify, HTTP download of frame binaries into PSRAM
  • Local button navigation across a deck of up to 8 stack pages plus a separate default/home page
  • KEY0 / KEY1 flip stack pages; KEY2 goes home (default.bin) and back; every 8th flip does a full refresh to limit ghosting

Publish pipeline

  • On the Raspberry Pi: HTML → Chromium → threshold → mono1 .bin
  • HTTP serves deck/*.bin and default.bin; MQTT topic epaper/display/image tells the panel which URLs to fetch
  • Wire format for the panel: 1872×1404 landscape mono1 (~328536 bytes/frame)

Repo

  • github.com/Cillian-Cooke/ink-port — flat firmware/ + pi/ layout

Facts above follow the Scratch Pad / ink-port reference (3 Nov 2026) and the ink-port README.

Trinity Five-A-Side

Fixtures, tables, and results for Trinity’s social football league.

I’m co-Lead Activator for Trinity Five-A-Side. I built the league site so organisers and players can open this week’s fixtures, standings, and results without digging through spreadsheets. The league has 66 teams in total — 8 leagues of 8, plus 2 extra teams in the Mixed Division.

Fixtures and results are read live from Google Sheets. The homepage opens on this week’s fixtures; tables, tournament, and team pages keep shareable deep links on Vercel.

Overview

  • 66 teams: 8 leagues of 8, plus 2 extra in the Mixed Division
  • Fixtures sheet and results sheet as source of truth, with a bundled seed fallback
  • React 19 + Vite 7 + React Router 7 on Vercel
  • Production at fiveside.vercel.app

What I do

I own the site end to end as co-Lead Activator: shipping features, keeping scores and fixtures current, and making sure the public pages match what the league runs each week.

  • App shell, routing, and Sheets-backed fixtures API
  • Tables, tournament, and team pages with OG-friendly deep links
  • Ongoing results publishing through the season

Atlas

A proximity-based social platform built solo.

Atlas is a social media app I’ve been building alone for over four months. No ads, no algorithmic feed, no bots. Content discovery works as a map of posts. Bluetooth Low Energy means you mostly see content from people you’ve actually been near.

Drag the interest tags: the coloured bubbles follow, the same interaction model as the Atlas map.

  • Stack: Flutter (Dart), Firebase, Bluetooth Low Energy
  • Solo: design, backend, frontend, architecture
